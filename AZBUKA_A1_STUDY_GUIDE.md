### Grammar and usage

**Ты vs Вы.** Use «ты» with friends and people your age when the situation is informal. Use «вы» for a stranger, an older person, or a polite interaction. «Как вас зовут?» is the safe formal question.

**Example:** Как вас зовут? — Меня зовут Анна. — What is your name? — My name is Anna.
**Common mistake:** Do not switch to «ты» with a stranger before the situation becomes informal.

### Grammar and usage

**Который час? vs Во сколько?.** Use «Который час?» to ask the current time. Use «Во сколько?» to ask when an event happens. A simple answer uses «В» plus the time: «В три часа».

**Example:** Во сколько встречаемся? — В три часа. — What time shall we meet? — At three o’clock.
**Common mistake:** Do not use the current-time question when you mean the time of an appointment.

**Example:** Это моя сестра. Она моя подруга. — This is my sister. She is my friend.
**Common mistake:** Do not use «мой» before a feminine noun: say «моя сестра».

## Study strategy

1. Learn the Russian chunk with its meaning before worrying about spelling.
2. Listen, then say the chunk aloud.
3. Use the dialogue variants as production targets; punctuation is not the goal.
4. Revisit recycled chunks in later stories and checkpoints.
5. Let the app scheduler prioritize new, due, and difficult items.
6. Finish with Unit 16 as a mixed survival mission rather than treating it as a vocabulary test.

## Course completion

The full 16-unit A1 spine is authored. The final capstone should be used to verify that you can greet, ask, navigate, transact, repair communication, describe needs, make plans, and handle simple problems in Russian.
